Welcome to Zista Inn

A quiet place to arrive.

Zista Inn is a comfortable stay in Walwati, near Shrivardhan and Aravi Beach in Maharashtra's Konkan region. It is a peaceful base for guests looking to enjoy the coast, local beaches and the relaxed pace of Konkan.

With comfortable Deluxe Rooms and a Family Room with Balcony, Zista Inn is suitable for couples, friends and families visiting the Shrivardhan area.

Where We Are

Stay near Aravi Beach and Shrivardhan.

Zista Inn is located in Walwati on the Aravi–Diveagar Road, near Shrivardhan in Maharashtra.

The location makes Zista Inn a convenient base for guests who want to explore Aravi Beach, Shrivardhan, Diveagar, Harihareshwar and the surrounding Konkan coastline.

If you're searching for a peaceful stay near Aravi Beach or looking for accommodation around Shrivardhan, Zista Inn offers a relaxed place to return to after exploring the coast.
