Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do Direct Booking Discounts work?

Direct weekday stays (Mon–Thu) start from ₹2,999/night, with our special Konkan Wednesday Escape at ₹2,499/night (Excludes public holidays & long holiday weekends). All direct bookings require a 50% advance confirmation.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Check-in: 12:00 PM (Noon) | Check-out: 10:00 AM. Early/late requests depend on room availability.

How far is the beach?

Aravi Beach is a 5-minute drive, and Shrivardhan Beach is 10 minutes away.

Is swimming pool access free?

Yes, pool access is complimentary for all registered staying guests. Synthetic/nylon swimwear is required.

Is there dining at the resort?

Complimentary in-room tea/coffee and paid breakfast is available. For lunch and dinner, authentic Konkani seafood and veg messes are just a 1 to 5-minute walk away.

Is there power backup?

Yes, inverter power backup runs essential lights, fans, and Wi-Fi during outages. (AC operates on the main power supply).

What is the alcohol & smoking policy?

Alcohol is strictly prohibited on resort premises. Smoking is allowed only in designated outdoor zones.

Zista Inn House Rules

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-in: 12:00 PM | Check-out: 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and extra charges.

Photo ID Requirement

All guests must present a valid government-approved photo ID at check-in.

Strictly No Alcohol

Alcohol is strictly prohibited across all resort grounds including rooms, balconies, and lawns.

Smoking Restrictions

Smoking is prohibited inside rooms and allowed only in designated open outdoor areas.

Quiet Hours (10:00 PM – 7:00 AM)

Loud music and disruptive noise are not permitted during quiet hours.

Power Outage Backup

Backup power operates lights and fans. Air conditioners run exclusively on the main electrical grid.

Pets & Damages

Pets are not allowed unless approved in advance. Guests are responsible for any damages caused to property.
